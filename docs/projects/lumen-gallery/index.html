Lumen Gallery

Client
Lumen Contemporary
Year
2025
Location
Seattle, Washington

Seasonal art direction for a contemporary gallery — each exhibition gets a light condition, a colour temperature and a type treatment of its own.

Lumen shows four exhibitions a year and wanted each one to feel like walking into a different quality of light. Instead of a fixed campaign style, we designed a framework: the gallery’s name stays put while colour temperature, imagery and typography shift with the season’s work.

Spring opened cool and architectural; summer went overexposed and loud. Posters, invitations, wall text and the social campaign all run off the same grid, so the system is fast to produce but never looks templated.

Attendance for the first two seasons beat the gallery’s previous records, and the framework is now in its second year.

What was delivered: Campaign concepts, Poster and invitation design, Social and digital assets.
